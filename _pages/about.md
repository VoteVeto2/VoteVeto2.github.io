Hello, I am [Xinyu Yang(click for pronunciation)](https://name-coach.com/xinyu-yang-veto), feel free to call me Christopher/Chris. 

I research agents. I build agents. Also into the post-training side of LLMs.

Currently doing my master's in [statistics at KU Leuven](https://lstat.kuleuven.be/). I had my two years in Leuven, having been participated in a few hackathon events(building MLOps and agentic AI backend), and interned at APB on clustering Belgium nationwide dispensing data on diabete patients. Prior to this, I completed a double major in [economics](https://econet.zjgsu.edu.cn/) and [statistics](http://tjjy.zjgsu.edu.cn/) at Zhejiang Gongshang University, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. [Bu Zhou](https://mypage.zjgsu.edu.cn/tjjy/zb2/main.htm) exploring the field of [nonparametric statistics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/highdimensional-statistics/8A91ECEEC38F46DAB53E9FF8757C7A4E). 


These days, I am more inclined to answer these questions, and building stuff: 

1. [Agentic RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02547), more specifically, the [*Verl*](https://github.com/volcengine/verl) framework and [RLHF based agents](https://github.com/langfengQ/verl-agent); 
2. [Evals for agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents); 
3. [Synthetic Data](https://github.com/pengr/LLM-Synthetic-Data), focus on application on tool use applications. 

I enjoy [long, unplanned wandering](https://bun.com/blog/bun-joins-anthropic#the-walk) around the city/river/canal during my free time. I am also open to a casual 30-min coffee chat, you can schedule a Google Meet w/ my Gmail at the bottom, or you can visit Shanghai/Hangzhou and let's find a good café together, preferably some [Panama washed Geisha](https://bestofpanama.auction/auction-2024).
