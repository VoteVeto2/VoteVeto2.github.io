## Message Box 

- **Issue**:  The current approach to diabete care is passive, only addressing problems after they have already developed. 
- **Problem**: Diabete Patients record are heterogenous, i.e. every patient is unique.
- **Benefits**: This allows doctors to give personalized care to the patients who need it most before they get sicker. 
- **So what**: Fewer emergency room visits, saving money for the government and improve the quality of life for patients. 
- **Solution**: We use novel modelling technqiues to predict their future behavior, based on pharmarcy records.
